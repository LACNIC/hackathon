Hackathon Platform (LACNIC44 - LACNOG 2025)

Welcome to LACNIC. As part of LACNIC44 - LACNOG 2025 in San Salvador, El Salvador, the hackathon platform will be available to all participants.

Important: this edition will not feature a formal hackathon, but you will still be able to access the platform, complete challenges, and earn points as part of the event experience.

About this edition

Unlike previous editions, LACNIC44 will not have a specific agenda or in-person hackathon activities. However, participants will still be able to explore the platform and participate individually or in teams.

You can test your knowledge in key areas such as security, bulk operations, MiLACNIC services, and policy compliance, while providing feedback to help improve the tools available to the community.

Challenges on the Platform

The platform offers activities in the following categories:

  • Policy Compliance: Evaluate and strengthen regulatory compliance processes.
  • Security: Reinforce security for accounts and services.
  • Membership: Explore and take advantage of LACNIC membership benefits.
  • Services: Test and suggest improvements for MiLACNIC.
  • Tools/Betas: Experiment with new beta features.
  • Bulk Operations: Optimize the use of large-scale management tools.
  • Feedback: Share comments to drive improvements.

Participation

All attendees of LACNIC44 - LACNOG 2025 can participate. You only need your MiLACNIC account to start completing challenges.

Benefits

  • Explore LACNIC services in a practical way.
  • Earn points and compare your performance in the ranking.
  • Provide valuable feedback to improve our tools.

Requirements

You only need your laptop or mobile device, a MiLACNIC account, and the motivation to explore.

Contact

If you have questions, email us at hackathon@lacnic.net or reach out through our social media.
